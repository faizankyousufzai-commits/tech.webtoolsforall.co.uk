You follow our guides at your own risk. Before you change a setting, delete a file, uninstall an app, or reset a device, back up your important data first. Photos, contacts, documents, and messages can be hard or impossible to recover once they are deleted. Check that your backup worked before you delete the original files.

In that case, check the official help pages for your device, or ask a local repair shop for help.

Our guides do not promise a fixed result for every device. A slow phone may improve after a cleanup, or it may still be slow because of old hardware, a weak battery, or a fault that needs a repair.
