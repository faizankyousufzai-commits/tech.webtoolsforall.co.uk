COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Tech WebTools guides free?

Yes. Every guide on Tech WebTools is free to read, with no account and no sign up.

Do I need to install anything to follow a guide?

No. Most guides use settings that are already on your phone or computer. When a guide needs an app, it says so clearly and it never asks you to download files from an unknown site.

Are the guides safe for beginners?

Yes. The steps are written in plain English for beginners, in order, with a warning before any step that deletes files or changes an important setting.

Can I ask for a guide on a topic I need?

Yes. Use the contact page to suggest a topic or report a mistake in a guide, and include the phone or computer model and the exact problem you see.
