How to Clear Cache on Firestick (Step-by-Step Guide)

By Faizan Khan Yousufzai | 9 November 2026 | Last updated 9 November 2026 | 6 min read

If you want to learn how to clear cache on firestick, the good news is that it takes only a few minutes and it is safe. Cache is a set of small temporary files that apps store so they can load faster next time. Over weeks of streaming, those files pile up, and a full cache is one of the most common reasons a Firestick starts to buffer, lag, or crash. Clearing it gives the app a clean start without deleting the app or signing you out.

This guide shows you how to clear cache from fire stick apps one by one, which apps cause the most trouble, and what to do if clearing the cache does not fix the problem. The steps work on every Fire TV Stick, including Firestick Lite, Firestick 4K, and Firestick 4K Max.

What is cache, and why does it slow down your Firestick?

When you open Netflix, YouTube, or Prime Video, the app saves small files such as images, thumbnails, and bits of video data, so it can load faster next time. That is the cache doing its job. The problem is that the Firestick has very little storage, often only 8 GB, and the cache never cleans itself. After a few months, a single streaming app can hold hundreds of megabytes of cache. Menus get slow, videos buffer even on a fast connection, and apps close on their own. Clearing the cache removes only those temporary files, so the app runs much better afterwards.

How to clear cache on Firestick for one app

Fire OS does not have a button that clears the cache for every app at once, so you clear each app on its own. Start with the app that is giving you trouble, because one bad app is usually the cause. Follow these steps:

1. From the Firestick home screen, go to Settings. You will find it at the far right of the top menu, marked with a gear icon.
2. Select Applications.
3. Select Manage Installed Applications. You will see a list of every app on the stick, with the biggest apps at the top.
4. Pick the app you want to fix, for example Netflix or YouTube.
5. Select Clear cache. The cache size shown next to it should drop to a very small number.
6. Press the Home button to go back.

That is all there is to it. Open the app again and check whether it runs better. The first launch after clearing the cache can take a few seconds longer than usual, because the app has to rebuild its temporary files. If you are asking how do you clear cache on firestick for an app that is not in the list, check that the app is really installed, because only installed apps appear under Manage Installed Applications.

How to clear cache firestick for every app

To clear firestick cache across the whole device, repeat the steps above for each app in the list. It only takes about a minute per app, and you do not need to do them all. Start with the streaming apps you use every day, because they build up the most cache. Video apps such as Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, Disney Plus, and BBC iPlayer are the biggest cache builders. Next, clear the cache for the Silk browser if you use it, because browsers store a lot of temporary files too. Small utility apps rarely need it, so you can skip them.

Work down the list from the top, because Fire OS sorts the apps by size, and the biggest apps are the ones most likely to be full of cache. After each one, the cache number resets. When you have done the top five or six apps, you have usually freed enough space to make a real difference.

Fire stick how to clear cache for Netflix, YouTube and Prime Video

Most people only need to fix one or two apps. For Netflix, go to Settings, then Applications, then Manage Installed Applications, then Netflix, then Clear cache. Netflix keeps a large cache because it stores artwork and previews, so clearing it often fixes the spinning loading circle. For YouTube, the same path applies, and clearing it can fix videos that refuse to play or a home screen that will not refresh. For Prime Video, the path is the same again, and clearing the cache is the first fix to try when a film stops halfway or the app closes back to the home screen.

Clear cache is not Clear data: On the same app screen you will see both buttons. Clear cache removes temporary files only. Clear data resets the app completely, signs you out, and deletes your settings. Only use Clear data if clearing the cache did not work and you are ready to sign in again.

What to do if clearing the cache does not fix it

Clearing the cache fixes most slow Firestick problems, but not all of them. If the app is still slow, work through these fixes in order. First, restart the Firestick. Hold the Select and Play buttons on the remote together for about ten seconds, or unplug the stick for 30 seconds and plug it back in. Second, force stop the app. On the same Manage Installed Applications screen, select the app and choose Force stop, then open it again. Third, check your storage. Go to Settings, then My Fire TV, then About, then Storage. If less than 1 GB is free, uninstall apps you do not use, because a nearly full Firestick will stay slow no matter how often you clear the cache.

Fourth, check your Wi-Fi. Buffering is often the internet, not the stick. Run a speed test on your phone in the same room, and restart your router if the speed is far below what you pay for. Fifth, update the app and Fire OS. Go to Settings, then My Fire TV, then About, then Check for Updates, and install anything it finds. If none of that helps, uninstall the app and install it again from the Amazon Appstore. A fresh install removes every leftover file. Only factory reset as a last step, because it erases everything and you have to set the stick up from scratch.

How often should you clear the cache?

Once a month is enough for most people. If you stream every evening, clear the cache for your two or three most used apps every two to three weeks. You do not need to do it every day, because the cache is useful when it is small. A simple habit is to clear the cache on the first day of each month, at the same time as you check for Fire OS updates. If you ever see a storage warning on screen, clear the cache straight away, because that warning means the stick is nearly full.

Clearing cache firestick apps regularly is a small job that prevents bigger problems. A Firestick with a clean cache starts apps faster, buffers less, and crashes less often, and you never lose a login by doing it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you clear cache on Firestick?

Go to Settings, then Applications, then Manage Installed Applications, pick the app, and choose Clear cache. Repeat for each app that is slow or buffering.

Does clearing cache on Firestick delete my apps or logins?

No. Clear cache only removes temporary files. Your apps stay installed and you stay signed in. Clear data is different, because it signs you out and resets the app.

How often should I clear the Firestick cache?

Once a month is enough for most people. Clear it sooner if an app buffers, crashes, or runs slowly, or if the Firestick warns you that storage is low.

Why is my Firestick still slow after I clear the cache?

Restart the Firestick, force stop the app, and check your Wi-Fi speed. If storage is still nearly full, uninstall apps you do not use or clear the data of one heavy app.
