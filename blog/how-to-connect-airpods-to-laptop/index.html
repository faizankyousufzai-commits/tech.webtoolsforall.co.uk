How to Connect AirPods to a Laptop (Windows & Mac, Step by Step)

By Faizan Khan Yousufzai | 9 November 2026 | Last updated 9 November 2026 | 7 min read

If you want to learn how to connect airpods to laptop, you do not need any extra app or cable. AirPods are normal Bluetooth headphones, so they pair with almost any laptop that has Bluetooth, whether it runs Windows or macOS. This guide shows you how to connect airpods to laptop on Windows 11, Windows 10, and Mac, and how to fix the most common problems: no sound, a mic that does not work, and AirPods that refuse to show up.

The steps work for AirPods, AirPods Pro, and AirPods Max, including the newest model, so if you searched for how to connect airpods pro 3 to laptop, the same steps apply.

Before you start: get your AirPods ready to pair

Do these three checks first. First, charge the AirPods. Put both AirPods in the case and check it has charge. If the light is amber, charge it for a few minutes first. Second, keep the AirPods in the case with the lid open while you pair. Third, turn Bluetooth on. On Windows, check Bluetooth is switched on in Settings. On a Mac, check the Bluetooth icon in the menu bar.

If your AirPods are already connected to your iPhone, they may jump back to the phone while you pair. Turn off Bluetooth on your iPhone for a minute, or move the phone to another room.

How to connect AirPods to a Windows 11 laptop

Windows 11 has the quickest setup. Follow these steps in order:

1. Put both AirPods in the charging case and open the lid.
2. Press and hold the setup button on the back of the case until the light on the front flashes white. This puts the AirPods in pairing mode. If the light flashes amber instead, keep holding the button until it turns white.
3. On the laptop, open Settings, then Bluetooth & devices.
4. Click Add device, then choose Bluetooth.
5. Wait for AirPods to appear in the list, then click it.
6. Wait for the message that says your device is ready, then click Done.

Play a video or a song to check the sound. If the sound still comes from the laptop speakers, click the speaker icon in the taskbar and pick AirPods as the output device. Next time, open the case near the laptop and Windows 11 usually reconnects on its own.

How to connect AirPods to a Windows 10 laptop

The steps on Windows 10 are almost the same, with slightly different menu names. Put the AirPods in the case, open the lid, and hold the setup button until the light flashes white. Then open Settings, then Devices, then Bluetooth & other devices. Make sure Bluetooth is on, click Add Bluetooth or other device, choose Bluetooth, and pick AirPods from the list.

If the AirPods do not appear, click the Bluetooth icon in the taskbar and choose Add a Bluetooth Device instead. Older Windows 10 laptops sometimes need a Bluetooth driver update first, so open Device Manager, find Bluetooth, right click your Bluetooth adapter, and choose Update driver.

How to connect AirPods to a Mac laptop

Pairing AirPods with a Mac is the easiest of all. Put the AirPods in the case, open the lid, and hold the setup button until the light flashes white. On the Mac, click the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Bluetooth, and click Connect next to your AirPods.

If you use the same Apple ID on your Mac and your iPhone, you may not need to pair at all. Open the case near the Mac and the AirPods can appear in the Bluetooth menu at the top of the screen, ready to connect with one click. On Windows you pair by hand each time you switch devices.

How to pair AirPods to laptop: what pairing mode looks like

People get stuck most often on the setup button. The setup button is a small round button on the back of the charging case. On AirPods Max, hold the noise control button on the right ear cup until the light flashes white. When you hold the button, the light first turns amber, then flashes white after a few seconds, which means the AirPods are ready to pair. If the light stays solid green, keep holding the button. If it flashes amber and never turns white, the case battery may be too low, so charge it and try again.

Do not press the button once: A single quick press only shows the battery level. You need to press and hold the button for several seconds until the light flashes white. This is the step most people miss.

How to connect my AirPods to my laptop when they will not show up

If the AirPods never appear in the Bluetooth list, work through these fixes. First, make sure the case lid is open and the light is flashing white. Second, move the case close to the laptop, within a metre, until pairing finishes. Third, turn Bluetooth off and on again on the laptop. Fourth, remove old pairings. If the AirPods were paired with this laptop before, choose Remove device or Forget in the Bluetooth settings, and pair them fresh.

Fifth, reset the AirPods. Put them in the case, close the lid for 30 seconds, open it, then hold the setup button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber and then white. Sixth, restart the laptop, because a restart clears the Bluetooth service, which sometimes gets stuck after a failed pairing. Then try the pairing steps again.

Fix no sound, bad mic, and other common problems

Connected but no sound is the most common complaint after pairing. The fix is usually the output device. On Windows, click the speaker icon in the taskbar, click the small arrow next to the volume slider, and pick AirPods. On a Mac, click the sound icon in the menu bar and pick AirPods. Also check the volume in the app you are using, because some apps have their own volume.

If the sound works but the mic does not, check the input device in the sound settings. On Windows, go to Settings, then System, then Sound, and pick AirPods under Input. On a Mac, go to System Settings, then Sound, then Input, and pick AirPods. On Windows, AirPods sound quality drops when the mic is in use, because Bluetooth switches to a lower quality mode for calls. It goes back to full quality when the call ends.

If only one AirPod plays sound, put both AirPods back in the case, close the lid for 30 seconds, then try again. Clean the speaker mesh gently with a dry cloth if one side sounds quiet. If the AirPods keep disconnecting, move closer to the laptop and turn off Bluetooth on nearby phones.

So can you connect airpods to pc and use them every day? Yes. Once they are paired, they work like any other Bluetooth headphones for music, videos, and calls. The only real difference from using them with an iPhone is that you connect them by hand on Windows, and the sound quality drops a little during calls.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my AirPods to my laptop?

Put the AirPods in the case, hold the setup button until the light flashes white, then add a Bluetooth device in your laptop settings and pick the AirPods from the list.

Can you connect AirPods to a PC?

Yes. AirPods work with any Windows 10 or 11 PC that has Bluetooth. They pair like normal Bluetooth headphones, and you can use them for sound and calls.

Can I connect AirPods to a laptop and my iPhone at the same time?

AirPods connect to one device at a time for sound, but they can switch between your Apple devices on their own. On a Windows laptop you need to connect them by hand each time you switch.

Why are my AirPods connected to my laptop but there is no sound?

Check that the AirPods are picked as the output device in your sound settings, turn the volume up, and disconnect any other Bluetooth speaker. If that fails, remove the AirPods and pair them again.
