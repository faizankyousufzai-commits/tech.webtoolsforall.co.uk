How to Speed Up a Slow MacBook: 15 Fixes That Actually Work

By Faizan Khan Yousufzai | 8 November 2026 | Last updated 8 November 2026 | 7 min read

If you want to learn how to speed up macbook performance, start with the free fixes in this guide. A slow Mac is usually caused by a full disk, too many apps running at once, or old software, and all three can be fixed at home without a repair shop. Work through the 15 fixes in order, and test your Mac after the first five, because most people see the difference before they reach the end.

Why is my Mac so slow?

When people ask why is my mac so slow, the answer is rarely one single fault. A Mac slows down when its disk is nearly full, because macOS needs free space to work, and when too many apps run at the same time, because they share the same memory. Old software can also slow things down, and a weak battery on an older Mac can make the processor run slower to save power. If you also wonder why is my macbook so slow at startup, the usual cause is apps that open every time you log in. The fixes below deal with each cause in turn, from the easiest to the most involved.

A mac running slow is not always a sign of a dying computer. If your Mac is less than six years old, a cleanup and an update will usually bring it back to normal speed. If it is older than that, the same steps still help, but do not expect it to feel like a brand new machine.

15 fixes to speed up your Mac

1. Restart your Mac

Restarting clears temporary files and frees memory. Click the Apple menu, choose Restart, and leave the Mac alone for a minute after it boots. If you have not restarted in weeks, this one step alone can make a mac computer running slow feel fast again, because it closes hidden processes you forgot were running.

2. Free up storage space

Open Apple menu, then About This Mac, then More Info, then Storage Settings. macOS shows what is using the most space. Aim to keep at least 10 percent of your disk free, and at least 20 GB on a small disk. Delete large video files you no longer need, move photos to an external drive, and empty the Bin.

3. Close apps that use the most memory

Open Activity Monitor from Spotlight search, click the Memory tab, and sort by usage. The apps at the top are using the most memory. Quit the ones you do not need, especially web browsers with dozens of open tabs.

4. Remove login items

Apps that open at login slow down every startup. Open System Settings, then General, then Login Items, and remove anything you do not need the moment the Mac starts. Keep only security software and cloud sync tools.

5. Update macOS and your apps

Open System Settings, then General, then Software Update, and install any update it offers. Then open the App Store and update your apps too.

6. Clear cache files safely

Cache files help apps load fast, but old cache can grow huge and cause problems. Clear your browser cache from the browser settings, and clear app cache only for apps you recognise. Do not delete system folders by hand. If you are unsure, use the storage recommendations in System Settings instead.

7. How to fix slow boot Mac

If you need to know how to fix slow boot mac startup, check three things in order. First, remove login items as in fix 4. Second, free up disk space as in fix 2, because a nearly full disk makes every boot slower. Third, disconnect external drives and USB devices before you start the Mac, then plug them in after it boots, because the Mac checks each one during startup.

8. Reduce transparency and motion

Visual effects use the graphics chip, and turning them down helps older Macs feel faster. Open System Settings, then Accessibility, then Display, and turn on Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency.

9. Check your battery health

On a MacBook, a worn battery can make the processor slow down to protect itself. Open System Settings, then Battery, then Battery Health, and check the condition. If it says Service Recommended, a new battery can restore full speed.

10. Close unused browser tabs and extensions

Each open tab uses memory, and each extension adds work to every page you load. Close tabs you have not used in a day, bookmark the ones you want to keep, and remove extensions you do not recognise from your browser settings.

11. Empty the Bin and remove large files

Deleted files still take up space until you empty the Bin. Right click the Bin in the Dock and choose Empty Bin, then check the Downloads folder for installers and videos you no longer need.

12. Restart in Safe Mode to find the problem

Safe Mode starts the Mac with only the essential software, which helps you find out if an app is causing the slowdown. On Apple silicon, hold the power button until the startup options appear, then hold Shift and click Continue in Safe Mode. On an Intel Mac, hold Shift while it starts.

13. Reindex Spotlight search

If search feels slow or the Mac fan runs all the time, Spotlight may be reindexing a large drive. Open System Settings, then Siri and Spotlight, then Spotlight Privacy, and drag your whole disk into the list. Wait a minute, remove it again, and Spotlight will rebuild its index.

14. Check Wi-Fi and iCloud sync

A Mac can feel slow when it is busy syncing files to iCloud or downloading a large update in the background. Click the iCloud icon in the menu bar or check System Settings to see if a sync is running. Pause large downloads while you work, and let them finish overnight.

15. Back up and reinstall macOS as a last resort

If nothing above helped, back up everything with Time Machine, then reinstall macOS from Recovery Mode. This gives you a clean system without deleting your files if you choose the reinstall option. It takes an hour or more, so only do it when you have time and a full backup.

How to speed up Mac performance every month

Once your Mac feels fast again, keep it that way with a short monthly routine. Restart once a week, keep at least 10 percent of the disk free, update macOS when Apple releases a fix, and check Activity Monitor when the fan starts running for no clear reason. If you wonder how can i speed up my mac computer without buying anything, this routine is the answer, because it costs nothing and it prevents the slowdown from coming back. If you also ask how do i speed up my mac after a big update, give it a day first, because macOS reindexes files after an update and that work ends on its own. The goal of this guide is simple, and it is the same as the question how to speed up mac performance in general: free space, close what you do not use, and keep the software current.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I speed up my Mac computer for free?

Restart it, free up storage, close heavy apps and remove login items. These four free steps fix most slow Macs, and they cost nothing but a few minutes.

How do I speed up my Mac if it is slow to start?

Remove apps that open at login, free up at least 10 percent of your disk, and update macOS. A slow boot is usually caused by login items or a nearly full disk.

Is it safe to delete cache files on a Mac?

Yes, if you only delete app cache and browser cache. Do not delete system files you do not recognise. When in doubt, use the built-in storage tools instead of deleting folders by hand.

When should I reinstall macOS to fix a slow Mac?

Only as a last resort, after you have tried storage cleanup, updates and login item removal. Back up with Time Machine first, because reinstalling can take an hour or more.
