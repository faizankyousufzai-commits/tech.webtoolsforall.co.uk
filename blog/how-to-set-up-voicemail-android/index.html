How to Set Up Voicemail on Android

By Faizan Khan Yousufzai | 8 November 2026 | Last updated 8 November 2026 | 6 min read

Learning how to set up voicemail android takes about five minutes, and it means you never miss an important message when you cannot answer a call. This guide works for Samsung, Pixel and any other Android phone, and it covers the Phone app method, the carrier method, greeting and PIN setup, and how to check and turn off voicemail later. The exact menus vary a little by phone and carrier, but the order of the steps is the same everywhere.

Voicemail is run by your mobile carrier, not by the phone itself, so the setup screen can look different on EE, O2, Vodafone or Three, and some phones show a voicemail tab while others do not. If one method below does not match your phone, try the next one.

How do I set up voicemail on Android phone: the Phone app method

To answer how do i set up voicemail on android phone with the Phone app, open the Phone app first. Tap the Voicemail tab at the bottom of the screen. If this is your first time, the phone will show a setup screen. Tap Set Up or Get Started, then follow the prompts. The phone will ask you to record a greeting and choose a PIN, and it may play a short test message so you can check that it works.

Some phones ask you to call your own number during setup. If you see that prompt, press and hold the 1 key on the dial pad, or call your own mobile number from the phone itself. The carrier voice menu will guide you through the same steps: record your name and greeting, choose a PIN, and confirm.

Tip: Choose a PIN you will remember but that is not your date of birth or 1234. You will need the PIN when you check voicemail from another phone.

How to set up voicemail message on Android

Your greeting is the first thing callers hear, so keep it short and clear. To learn how to set up voicemail message on android, open the Phone app, tap the three dots in the top corner, then Settings, then Voicemail, then Greeting. Tap Record a new greeting, speak slowly, and save it when you are happy with it.

Some phones offer a default greeting that only reads out your number. That works, but a personal greeting sounds more professional and tells callers they reached the right person.

Set a voicemail PIN and notifications

During setup, your carrier will ask you to choose a PIN. This PIN protects your messages when you check voicemail from another phone, so do not use a simple number like 0000 or 1111. After setup, turn on voicemail notifications so you know when a message arrives. Open Settings on your phone, then Notifications, then the Phone app, and make sure voicemail alerts are on. You can choose a sound and a badge on the app icon, so a new message is easy to spot.

Samsung voicemail notes

On a Samsung phone, open the Phone app and tap the Voicemail tab, or press and hold 1 on the dial pad. Samsung phones on UK carriers usually support visual voicemail, which shows a list of messages you can tap to play. If the tab is missing, open Phone settings by tapping the three dots, then Settings, then Voicemail, and check that your carrier is selected.

Pixel voicemail notes

On a Pixel phone, open the Phone app and tap Voicemail at the bottom. Pixel phones offer voicemail transcription in some regions, which shows the message as text. If transcription is not available on your carrier, you can still listen to the audio as normal. Pixel users can also turn on Call Screen for unknown numbers, which asks the caller to state their reason before the phone rings.

How to check voicemail on Android

Once setup is done, it is easy to learn how to check voicemail on android. Open the Phone app and tap the Voicemail tab to see your messages. Tap a message to play it, and use the on screen controls to pause, delete or call the person back. If your phone has no voicemail tab, press and hold 1 on the dial pad to call your voicemail, then enter your PIN when asked. You can also call voicemail from another phone by calling your own number, waiting for the greeting, and pressing star or hash when prompted.

Delete old messages you no longer need, because most carriers only store a limited number of messages, and some delete them after 30 days. Visual voicemail lets you delete one message at a time without listening to the whole list in order.

How to turn off voicemail on Android

Some people do not want voicemail at all, and they want to know how to turn off voicemail on android. Open the Phone app, tap the three dots, then Settings, then Voicemail, and look for a switch or a deactivate option. On many UK carriers there is no switch in the phone settings, because voicemail is a carrier service. In that case, the reliable way is to ask your carrier to turn it off. You can also use call forwarding: open Phone settings, tap Calls, then Call forwarding, and forward unanswered calls to a number that does not take messages. Test it by calling your number from another phone after you change the setting.

How to disable voicemail on Android

People also ask how to disable voicemail on android when the settings menu hides the option. The steps are the same as turning it off: check the Phone app first, then contact your carrier if there is no switch. Some people type how disable voicemail android into search when they mean the same thing, and the answer is the same. Carriers in the UK can disable voicemail on your line, usually for free, and you can ask them to turn it back on later if you change your mind. Another question that comes up is how do i disable voicemail on android without calling my carrier. On some networks you can dial ##002# followed by the call button to cancel all call forwarding, including voicemail.

How do you set up voicemail on an Android

If you still wonder how do you set up voicemail on an android after trying the steps above, check two things. First, make sure your SIM is active and your plan includes voicemail. A new SIM can take a few hours before voicemail works. Second, restart your phone once and try again, because the Phone app sometimes needs a restart to show the voicemail tab after a SIM change. If it still does not work, call your carrier and ask them to check that voicemail is active on your line. They can often fix it in a minute, and they can also reset your PIN if you are locked out.

Voicemail is a small feature, but it saves missed calls from turning into missed chances. Set it up once, record a clear greeting, and check it at least once a day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up voicemail on an Android phone for the first time?

Open the Phone app, tap the Voicemail tab, and follow the setup prompts to record a greeting and set a PIN. If your carrier asks you to call your own number first, do that and follow the voice prompts.

Why is there no voicemail tab on my Android phone?

Some carriers do not support visual voicemail, so the tab does not appear. In that case, press and hold 1 on the dial pad to call voicemail, or call your own number and follow the prompts.

How do I turn off voicemail on Android?

You cannot always turn voicemail off in settings. Open the Phone app, go to Settings, then Voicemail, and look for a switch. If there is none, call your carrier or use call forwarding to a number that does not take messages.

Is voicemail free on Android?

Listening to voicemail is usually free in the UK on most plans, but calling voicemail from abroad can cost extra. Check your carrier plan if you travel, and use visual voicemail over Wi-Fi when you can.
